# Strategize

Use for a group's TASTE strategy conversation or revision. Read [components](references/components.md) for distinctions and [facilitation](references/facilitation.md) for the working method. Follow their links to Karl Scotland's posts when a distinction matters.

Start with purpose, current situation, and available source material. Synthesize a few draft statements with rationale and uncertainty; ask each participant to correct, reject, or add to them. Mark each item agreed, candidate, or unresolved. Do not infer consensus from silence. Keep the diagnosis of the critical addressable challenge or opportunity visible before settling on strategy.

Develop the components in Karl's preferred order, revisiting them as the group learns. Discuss correlations in words as soon as both sides exist. Draw a matrix only when the group wants the format and the pattern of relationships is useful to examine. Shared understanding and decisions matter more than a polished template.

Use even-over language only for two genuinely desirable outcomes and a consequential choice. A forced tradeoff is counterproductive; a strategy can be expressed plainly. Never present a correlation as causal proof.

Private workbooks, Google Docs, and live transcripts may inform authorized session drafts. Do not reproduce their contents in this skill or its references. Keep generalized public method separate from the group's decisions. Edit the group's working artifact only when authorized.
